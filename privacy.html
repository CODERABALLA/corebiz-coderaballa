Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CoderAballa collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit our website or use our web development services in Kisii, Nairobi and across Kenya.

1. Introduction

When you contact us or use our services, you are trusting us with your information. We take that seriously and only collect what we need to respond to you and deliver good work.

This policy is meant to help you understand what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can ask us to update, export or delete it. We handle personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to answer your enquiries and provide our services. The types of information we collect include:

2.1 Information You Provide

When you fill in our contact form, subscribe to our newsletter or work with us on a project, you may give us:

  • Your name and contact details, such as email and phone number
  • The subject and content of your message or project brief
  • Your newsletter email address
  • Business details and files you share so we can build your website or app

2.2 Automatic Information

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information when you visit:

  • Device type and browser settings
  • IP address and approximate region
  • Pages visited and time of visit

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to run our business and look after our clients. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Reply to your enquiries and prepare quotes
  • Plan, build, deliver and support your project
  • Send newsletters and updates, only if you subscribed
  • Keep our website secure and working properly
  • Improve our website and services

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with others in the following cases:

4.1 With Your Consent

We will share personal information with other people or organisations when you have agreed to it, for example when a project needs a third-party tool or service that you have approved.

4.2 Service Providers

We use trusted third parties to run our website and projects, such as hosting providers, GitHub for code storage, and Google Fonts for typography. These providers may process limited technical data on our behalf.

4.3 For Legal Reasons

We will share personal information if we have a good-faith belief that it is reasonably necessary to:

  • Meet any applicable law, regulation, legal process or lawful government request
  • Enforce our agreements with clients
  • Detect, prevent or address fraud, security or technical issues
  • Protect the rights, property or safety of our clients and users

5. Data Security

We work hard to protect your information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. In particular:

  • We use HTTPS to encrypt data sent between your browser and our website
  • We keep project files and credentials in secured accounts
  • We limit access to personal information to the people who need it to do their work

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we do our best to protect your data.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to be informed about how your data is used
  • The right to access your personal information
  • The right to correct inaccurate information
  • The right to request deletion of your information
  • The right to object to or restrict our processing of your information

You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time, and you can email us to exercise any of these rights.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.

Your continued use of our website after any changes means you accept the updated policy.
