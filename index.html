Building Useful Software From Kisii to Nairobi

CoderAballa is a web development studio run by a final-year Computing student at Kisii University who builds real websites and applications for real people, not just class projects.

We work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js, Python, Java and PostgreSQL, and we use Git and GitHub workflows on every project so your code is organised, safe and easy to maintain.

Our work spans business websites, institutional sites, quiz and learning platforms, and data-driven tools. Whether you are a shop in Kisii or a startup in Nairobi, we keep things simple: understand your goal, build it well, and hand it over with clear documentation.

F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

01

Where are you based and who do you work with?

We are based in Kisii and Nairobi, and we work with clients anywhere in Kenya, in person or remotely. Small businesses, schools, churches, startups and individuals are all welcome.

02

How long does it take to build a website?

A simple website usually takes one to two weeks. A full business site takes two to four weeks, and custom web applications depend on the features you need. We agree on a timeline before we start.

03

Which technologies do you use?

HTML, CSS, JavaScript and TypeScript on the front end, with React, Next.js and Bootstrap. On the back end we use Python, Java and Bun with PostgreSQL databases, and we manage everything with Git and GitHub.

04

How do I pay, and can I pay in stages?

We accept M-Pesa and bank transfer. Most projects are split into a deposit to start, a middle payment at review stage, and the balance on delivery.

05

Do you offer support after the website goes live?

Yes. Our monthly plans include updates, bug fixes and security checks, and you always own your code, which lives in a GitHub repository you can access.
